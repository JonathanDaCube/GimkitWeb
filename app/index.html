Gimkit Community App

Keep up with community meetings, even when the website is closed. Get Windows notifications when a meeting starts.

Download for Windows

Windows x64 · Free · No account or .NET installation required

Meeting alerts

See notifications for new active meetings and scheduled meetings when they start.

Runs in the background

The app checks approximately once a minute while you are signed in to Windows.

Ready at sign-in

Open the app once to start the checker and register it to start when you sign in.

Get started

  1. Download the ZIP file and extract all its contents to a folder you want to keep, such as Documents.
  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click gimkitapp.exe. Keep the other extracted files beside it.
  3. Read the welcome message. The app starts the background checker and enables startup for your Windows account.

The first check ignores meetings already in progress. Notifications require Windows to be awake, your account to be signed in, and notifications to be enabled. Do Not Disturb may hide alerts.

To stop or check the app, open a terminal in its folder and run gimkitapp stop or gimkitapp query. Stopping it does not disable startup; use Windows Startup settings to do that.
